City Contacts

Utilities Change From Town To Town

Each town runs its own water and sewer utility, and the city side of a leak belongs to the city. In Lehi, Water and Sewer is at (385) 201-1700 in office hours, with 801-836-1045 listed for after hours emergencies. For other towns, check that city website for its current public works line.

Wastewater from Lehi, American Fork, Alpine, Highland, Pleasant Grove, Saratoga Springs and several other cities is treated by the Timpanogos Special Service District. That shared treatment does not change who owns the pipe outside your house, so ask your city where its main begins.

Not sure whose problem it is?

Call us anyway. We can usually tell from the meter, the shutoff and the symptoms whether a problem is on the city side or in your house, and we will not quote a repair on pipe that belongs to the city.
