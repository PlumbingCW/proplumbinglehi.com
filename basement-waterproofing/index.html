Diagnosis First

Find The Source Before You Pay For A Fix

Basement water has many sources and each needs a different answer. A leaking pipe is a plumbing job. Groundwater pushing through a wall or floor is a drainage job. A cracked foundation wall is a structural job.

We start by asking when water appears. After storms and snow melt, it is usually groundwater. After using the washing machine or shower, it is usually plumbing.

Parts Of The System

What A Waterproofing System Includes

Typical components

  • An interior perimeter drain that collects water at the footing
  • A sump pit and pump to move the water outside
  • A vapor liner on the wall or floor edge to guide water to the drain
  • Window well drains that connect to the system
  • A discharge line that ends away from the foundation

Not every basement needs every part. A small seep may need only a window well fix and a downspout extension. We recommend the smallest system that solves the problem.

Honest Limits

What We Do And What Needs A Different Pro

We handle plumbing causes and interior drainage systems. If the cause is a structural foundation crack or a settling wall, a structural contractor should evaluate it. We will say so on the phone or at the visit rather than sell you a system that cannot fix the problem.

Related Safety

Radon And The Sump Pit

Radon is a live concern in Utah, where the state average test result is 5.3 pCi per liter against an EPA action level of 4. Test the lowest living level for two to four days. If a basement gets a new drain and pit, ask a radon professional whether the pit lid and floor seams should be sealed.

Diagnosis

Where Basement Water Actually Comes From

Basement water usually has one of three sources. Surface water runs toward the foundation from poor grading or short downspouts. Groundwater rises under the slab after snow melt or heavy rain. A plumbing leak inside a wall or beneath the floor can look the same on a wet basement floor.

A plumber can rule out supply lines, drains and the water heater first. If those are dry, the fix moves to drainage, grading, gutters or a sump system. Matching the fix to the source matters, because a sump pit does nothing for a leaking supply pipe.
