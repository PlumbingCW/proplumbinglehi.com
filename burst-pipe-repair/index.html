Before We Arrive

If A Pipe Has Burst, Do These Four Things

1

Close the main valve

Turn the main water shutoff clockwise until it stops. It is often where the line enters the house or at the water heater.

2

Cut power near the water

Switch off breakers for wet rooms and appliances if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

3

Drain the lines

Open the lowest faucet and the highest faucet to relieve pressure and let water drain.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Tell us what you see, where the water is and what you shut off.

If you cannot find the main valve, close the valve at the meter box. Do not operate the city valve at the curb unless the city tells you to, and call Lehi Water and Sewer at (385) 201-1700 or 801-836-1045 after hours if you need help.

Freeze Risk

Winter Breaks In Lehi

Pipes in garages, exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated rooms are the first to freeze during hard cold. Outdoor hose bibs are at risk when a hose is left attached. If you have a stretch of cold weather coming, keep cabinet doors open under sinks on exterior walls and let a faucet drip.

Once the October 15 irrigation cutoff passes, owners shut off the pressurized irrigation connection. Draining outdoor lines and spigots at that time reduces the chance of a frozen break in the first deep freeze.

Process

How The Repair Goes

Confirm the shutoff

2

Locate the break

3

Repair or replace

4

If many pinholes appear along the same line, repeated repairs are a warning that the whole line is wearing out. A planned repiping visit may cost less than a string of emergency calls.

After The Repair

Insurance And Wet Materials

Many standard homeowner policies treat sudden and accidental water damage differently from slow leaks or wear. Your policy decides what is covered, so call your insurer early and take photos before anything is removed. We cannot tell you what your policy covers.

Wet drywall, insulation and carpet usually need drying and may need removal. We handle the pipe, and a restoration company handles the drying. We can tell you what we saw so you can describe it to both.
