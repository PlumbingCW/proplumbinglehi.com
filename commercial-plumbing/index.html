Compliance

Backflow Assemblies And Annual Testing

Utah amendments to the International Plumbing Code (IPC) require backflow assemblies to be tested when installed and within ten days of installation, and then tested every year by a Utah certified backflow assembly tester. Many commercial properties have irrigation or fire line assemblies that need this attention.

We can help you understand what your assemblies require. Ask the dispatcher when you call, and we will explain what a test visit involves.

Preventive Work

Grease Traps And Floor Drain Care

Kitchens that serve food depend on grease control. Traps and interceptors need regular service, and a floor drain that smells or drains slowly is an early warning. We can clean and inspect lines, and we will say when a pumping contractor is needed instead.

Working With Property Managers

Managers often need quick answers on cost and timing so tenants are not left waiting. We give a clear price before work starts and a plain explanation you can pass on. For leaks across a building, our leak detection service tests zone by zone and meter by meter.
