Prepare

What To Have Ready When You Call

The dispatcher will ask

  • The address and the nearest cross street
  • What is happening, such as a leak, a backup or no hot water
  • Whether you have shut off the water or power
  • The best number to reach you
  • Whether anyone has safety concerns, such as a gas smell

If you smell gas, leave the building before you call, then call 911 and the gas utility. Enbridge Gas lists 1-800-767-1689 for gas emergencies.
