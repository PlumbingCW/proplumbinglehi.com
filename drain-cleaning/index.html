Diagnosis

What A Clogged Drain Is Telling You

Where the clog sits decides the fix. One slow sink usually means a blockage in that fixture trap or its branch line. Several slow drains at the same time point to the main line, which is a bigger job.

A quick test helps. Run the nearest tub or shower and flush a toilet. If water gurgles up in a different fixture, the blockage is downstream of both, and our sewer line cleaning visit is the better starting point.

Signs the clog is in the main line

  • Water backs up in a shower or tub when you run the washing machine
  • Toilets gurgle when a sink drains
  • Floor drains in the basement or garage bubble or smell
  • Every drain slows down at once, not just one

Local Notes

What Lehi Homeowners Should Know About Their Lines

Lehi sewage is treated by the Timpanogos Special Service District, a shared system. The city owns the sewer main, and the pipe from your house to the main is part of your property line. Ask Lehi Water and Sewer at (385) 201-1700 where the city main begins if you are unsure who is responsible.

Parts of Lehi are on septic instead of city sewer. A backup in a septic home may be a full tank or a failed drain field, and cabling will not fix either. We will say so on the phone before anyone drives out.
