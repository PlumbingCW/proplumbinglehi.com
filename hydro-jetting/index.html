The Difference

What Jetting Does That A Cable Cannot

A cable machine cuts a hole through a blockage. That restores flow, but grease and scale often remain on the pipe wall and the line narrows again within months. Hydro jetting pushes water through a nozzle at high pressure and scours the entire inside of the pipe.

The result is a line that is cleaned, not just opened. For repeat clogs, jetting is often the better long term fix.

Cable machine

  • Cuts a channel through the blockage
  • Good for hair, paper and soft clogs
  • Leaves scale and grease on the wall
  • Faster and cheaper for simple clogs

Hydro jetting

  • Scours the full pipe wall
  • Cuts through grease, scale and small roots
  • Flushes debris out of the line
  • Best for repeat or heavy blockages

Emergency Response

Backed Up And Need It Cleared Tonight

Jetting is not always the first move in an emergency. For a sudden backup, the plumber usually opens the line with a cable and then decides whether jetting should follow. Call the 24/7 line and the dispatcher will explain which approach fits your situation.

Before the machine arrives

  • Keep people and pets out of any wet area
  • Do not pour chemicals into the drain
  • Clear access to the cleanout if you can reach it

Local Notes

Lehi Lines And Roots

Roots look for water and enter joints in sewer laterals, and the risk grows as trees mature around older and newer lots alike. Jetting can trim small roots, but a line with heavy root invasion may need a repair after the cleaning.

Septic homes in Lehi are a different case. We clear the house lines, but a full tank or failed drain field needs a pumper and a separate repair. We will tell you that on the phone.
