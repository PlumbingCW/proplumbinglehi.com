Before We Arrive

What To Do While You Wait For A Plumber

The first ten minutes of a plumbing emergency decide how much damage you get. These steps are the same for almost every house in Lehi, and none of them need tools.

1

Shut off the water

Turn the main shutoff valve clockwise until it stops. If you cannot find it, close the valve at the meter box.

2

Kill power near water

If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, switch off those breakers only if you can reach them while staying dry.

3

Clear the area

Move rugs, electronics and stored boxes away from the wet spot and open a drain or door if water is pooling.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Tell us what you see and hear. If you smell gas, leave the house first and call 911 and the gas utility.

Sewer backups are different. Stop using every drain in the house, keep children and pets out of the wet area and call right away. Sewage water carries bacteria, so wet carpet and drywall usually need more than a mop.

Lehi Homes And Water

Plumbing In A Town That Keeps Growing

Lehi was settled in the fall of 1850 as Sulphur Springs and took its current name in 1852. That history shows up in the housing. Older homes near Main Street sit a short drive from newer neighborhoods around Traverse Mountain and Thanksgiving Point, so pipe age and materials change from street to street. A good plumber asks the year your house was built before guessing at a cause.

Older homes deserve a second look before a repipe, because the city links pre 1986 construction to a higher chance of lead in house plumbing. Ask about lead testing first. Our repiping page explains how a replacement plan usually works.

The city supplies culinary water from wells, springs and wholesale surface water from the Central Utah Water Conservancy District, treated with chlorine. The water reports we reviewed do not publish a hardness number, so we do not quote one. If scale is building on fixtures, test your tap before you buy a softener, then read our softener and filtration page.

Culinary water and irrigation water are separate

Lehi runs a pressurized irrigation system that carries untreated water and is separate from the water inside your house. The city bars irrigation use after October 15, and owners turn their connection off by that date. A wet yard in November is a different problem from a wet basement, and the two need different fixes.

When the leak is on the city side of the meter, the city handles it. Lehi Water and Sewer can be reached at (385) 201-1700 during office hours, and the city lists 801-836-1045 for after hours emergencies. If the leak is on your side, call us and we will track it down with our leak detection service.

When To Fix It Yourself And When To Call
