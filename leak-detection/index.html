Warning Signs

Signs Water Is Escaping Somewhere You Cannot See

Most hidden leaks give hints before they cause real damage. A water bill that climbs with no change in use is the clearest one. Others include the sound of water running when everything is off, damp carpet, a musty smell and warm spots on a floor.

Common clues

  • A meter that keeps moving with every fixture off
  • A wet or stained ceiling, wall or baseboard
  • Cracks or warm spots in a slab floor
  • Low water pressure that comes on suddenly
  • A patch of lawn that stays wet or sinks
DIY Check

A Meter Test You Can Do Today

Turn off every tap and appliance that uses water, including the ice maker. Find your culinary water meter and read the dial or look for the small leak indicator that spins when water moves. Wait fifteen minutes and read it again.

If the reading changed, water is moving somewhere. Lehi also uses secondary irrigation meters, and a purple meter lid marks that irrigation meter, so check you are reading the culinary one. If you are unsure which is which, call and we will help you identify it.

Responsibility

Whose Side Of The Meter Is It

The water meter marks the handoff. A leak before the meter is the city side and Lehi Water and Sewer handles it at (385) 201-1700, or 801-836-1045 after hours. A leak after the meter is on your property and is yours to fix.

Our water line pages cover the repair side. See water service line repair for buried lines and burst pipe repair for lines inside the house.

Outdoor Water

Irrigation Leaks In Lehi

Lehi runs a pressurized irrigation system that is separate from the culinary water inside your house. The city bars irrigation use after October 15 and expects owners to turn off their connection by then. Secondary meters are being installed on all properties as part of a program that runs to 2030.

A wet yard in winter can mean a leak in the irrigation line or a valve left open. We can test and locate the leak, and we will tell you whether the fix is plumbing or an irrigation contractor job.

Bigger Properties

Apartments, Businesses And Pools

Multifamily buildings and commercial properties often have long pipe runs and several meters. Leaks there can hide for weeks and show up first as a big bill. We scope those jobs by building, check each meter and test zone by zone.

Pool and spa leaks are different. We can check the plumbing lines, but a leak in the pool shell needs a pool specialist, and we will say so if that is the case.

Safety Steps While You Wait

  • Shut off the main water valve if water is actively spreading
  • Turn off power at the breaker to any wet room before touching switches
  • Stay out of standing water that could reach outlets or cords
  • Move rugs and valuables out of the wet area
  • Take photos of the damage and the meter reading for your records
