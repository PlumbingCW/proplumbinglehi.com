City Overview

American Fork At A Glance

American Fork sits just north of Lehi along the I-15 and State Street corridors, with the mouth of American Fork Canyon and UT-92 to the east. The city counts about 38,000 residents and roughly 11,500 water connections, according to its 2024 state water conservation plan. That is a lot of houses with pipes of every age.

Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In American Fork

Water moves fast in a finished basement or a kitchen above a crawlspace. These four steps cut the damage while the plumber is on the way.

1

Shut off the main valve

Turn the main shutoff clockwise until it stops. It is usually where the water line enters the house.

2

Kill power to wet areas

Switch off breakers for the room only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

3

Clear the immediate area

Lift rugs, boxes and electronics away from the wet spot and open a drain if water is pooling.

4

Call our 24/7 line

Tell the dispatcher the street, what is leaking and what you have shut off.

Local Challenges

What Plumbing Looks Like In An American Fork Home

American Fork says much of its culinary distribution piping is more than 50 years old. That piping belongs to the city, but it affects you. Pressure swings and discolored water after main work are the signs, and when a whole street sees them, the city should hear about it first.

Inside the house, the age of the home matters more than the age of the city mains. Older neighborhoods around downtown may still have galvanized or aging copper lines, while newer subdivisions to the west are more likely to have PEX. A plumber should ask the build year before guessing.

The city also finished a pressurized irrigation system between 2007 and 2010 and reports that about 95 percent of residents are connected. Secondary meters are part of that system. If your yard is wet in November, the cause may be an irrigation line rather than the plumbing in the house.

Local Note

Water, Irrigation And The Canyon

The American Fork conservation plan describes culinary water from wells and springs, and the city treats irrigation water separately. Because of that split, a leak in the yard is not automatically a leak in the house. We check both before quoting a repair.

Timpanogos Cave National Monument sits up American Fork Canyon on UT-92. Homes near the canyon mouth deal with snow melt and runoff in spring, so sump pumps and window wells matter there. Our sump pump page covers the checks to make before the thaw.
