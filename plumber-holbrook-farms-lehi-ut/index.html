The Neighborhood

About Holbrook Farms

Holbrook Farms was a 686 acre parcel sold by the Holbrook family of Lehi. The Lehi Free Press reported in 2016 that Ivory Homes would develop the residential portion and the Boyer Company the commercial portion. The plans were approved by the city council shortly before that report.

The same article says the master plan included large park areas, greenway corridors and a neighborhood commercial core. It also listed a new fire station, water tank and power station as planned infrastructure. Those were plans at the time of the article, not a description of what each street looks like today.

Practical Notes

What To Know About Plumbing In A Newer Community

This section is general guidance, not a claim about any one house. A newer home is not automatically trouble free, and we do not assume a problem exists because of when a home was built. We look at the fixture, the valve or the line in front of us.

Many newer homes have a water heater and a pressure regulator that have never been serviced. Draining a few gallons from a tank water heater once a year clears sediment and helps you spot a leak early. Our water heater repair page explains the warning signs.

Water quality is another question owners ask. The city water reports we reviewed do not publish a hardness number, so we do not quote one. If you see white scale on fixtures, test your tap first, then read about softeners and filtration before buying.

Local Note

Warranty Work Versus A Service Call

If a home is still under a builder warranty, call the builder first for covered items. A plumber can still diagnose the problem and tell you whether a repair is a defect or ordinary wear. Keep any photos and dates for the warranty claim.

For items outside warranty, such as a clogged drain or a worn faucet cartridge, call us directly. We give a clear price before work starts.
