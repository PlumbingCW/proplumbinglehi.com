Practical Notes

Plumbing Notes For Newer Compact Homes

This section is general guidance, not a statement about any specific Inverness home. We do not assume a defect because a home is new or old. We start from what you describe and what we see on site.

Newer homes often have a smaller utility space, so a water heater sits close to the furnace and the main shutoff. Know where the shutoff is and test it once a year. A valve that has never been turned can stick when you need it.

If a fixture is still under a builder or manufacturer warranty, check that coverage first. A plumber can still diagnose the problem and tell you whether it looks like a defect or normal wear.

Local Note

Townhome Style Homes And Shared Lines

Where homes share walls, shared drain stacks or supply lines may belong to the HOA. Your documents decide who pays. If you are not sure, take photos of the leak and contact your management company.

We will tell you whether the line we are looking at serves only your unit, so you know who to ask about the repair.

Simple Steps

Quick Checks Before You Call

For a toilet that keeps running, lift the tank lid and look at the flapper and the fill valve. A flapper that does not seal is a common cause and a small part. If the tank is cracked or the base leaks, stop using the toilet and call.

For no hot water, check the breaker or the gas supply first. If the tank is leaking from the bottom, shut off the cold water supply to the heater and call the dispatch line. Do not keep refilling a leaking tank.
