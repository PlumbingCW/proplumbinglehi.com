About Ivory Ridge

Ivory Ridge is a residential community in north Lehi. Real estate listing data places most of its homes in the 2010s, with a median of about 3,100 square feet on small lots. Practical Notes

Plumbing Notes For Small Lot Homes

Everything in this section is general guidance. We make no claim about the pipes in any particular Ivory Ridge home. On compact lots, sewer cleanouts and water shutoffs can be close to a neighbor, so knowing where yours is saves time in an emergency.

Find your main shutoff and your sewer cleanout before you need them. Tag the shutoff so family members can find it fast. If you cannot find a cleanout, a technician can locate one during a drain visit.

Repeated clogs in more than one drain often mean a main line problem. A camera inspection shows whether the cause is a belly, roots or buildup. Our sewer inspection page explains what the camera shows.

Local Note

HOA Rules And Plumbing Work

Some HOAs require notice or approval for exterior work, such as a sewer repair that opens a front yard. Check your covenants before a planned repair. Emergency work to stop active flooding is normally handled first, then reported.

We do not give legal advice about your HOA. We will explain the plumbing side clearly so you can have an informed conversation with your management company.

Simple Steps

What To Tell The Dispatcher

Name the street, which fixtures are affected and when the problem started. If only one drain is slow, say which one. If water is backing up in a tub or floor drain when you run a washer, say that too, because it points to the main line.

If the HOA manages part of the property, mention it. We will explain what we find and what it means for the repair, so you can talk to your management company with the facts in hand.
