Common Project

Basement And Remodel Plumbing

Finishing a basement in Lehi often means adding a bathroom or wet bar. The drain has to slope, vent and tie into the main, and a floor may need to be broken. If the basement is below the sewer line, an ejector pump may be part of the plan.

We map the existing lines first so the new work fits. We also check how the project changes load on the main drain.

Gas Lines

Gas Line Work Starts With Safety

If you smell gas

  • Leave the building right away
  • Do not use switches, phones or open flames inside
  • Call 911 and the gas utility, Enbridge Gas lists 1-800-767-1689
  • Wait outside until the building is cleared

Gas line work covers new appliance hookups, line extensions and size checks for tankless water heaters. We confirm the load and the line size before quoting. Conversions between gas types need appliance specific parts, and we explain what that involves.

Preparation

Planning An Install Before The Plumber Arrives

Have the fixture on site before the visit and keep the box and the paper instructions. The model number tells us the rough in size, the water connections and what parts are included. A toilet, for example, has a rough in distance from the wall to the drain center, and the new one has to match the old one.

Clear the area under sinks and around the water heater, and tell us where the main shutoff is. Open access saves time and keeps the price down. If a fixture sits in a tight cabinet, mention it on the phone so the plumber brings the right tools.

An install is a good moment to replace old shutoff valves and braided supply lines. Those parts cost little compared with the damage a failed line can cause. We list them in the price before work begins so you can decide.

New fixtures and supply lines are installed to the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Utah amendments. If your project needs a permit, the city building office is the place to ask.
