Last updated November 12, 2026. This policy applies to proplumbinglehi.com and the phone line at (385) 488-1892. It is written for homeowners and business owners who contact Lehi Pro Plumbing for plumbing services.

Information We Collect

This website does not have contact forms or user accounts. When you call, we collect the information you give us, such as your name, address and the plumbing problem. If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you send.

Call Recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call after hearing the notice, you agree to the recording. We use recordings to check that information was captured correctly and to train staff.

How We Use Information

We use call and email information to schedule visits, give prices, perform the work and follow up on questions. We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the people who need it to complete your service and where the law requires it.

Third Party Services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows maps from Google Maps. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when the page loads. Their use of that information is covered by Google policies.

Cookies And Analytics

We do not intentionally set tracking cookies on this site. Third party services such as the embedded map may set their own cookies, and you can control those through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you by calling (385) 488-1892 or emailing contact@proplumbinglehi.com. You can also ask that a call not be recorded at the start of the call.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@proplumbinglehi.com or to Lehi Pro Plumbing, 4275 N Thanksgiving Way, Lehi, UT 84043.
