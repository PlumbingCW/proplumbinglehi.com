Warning Signs

Signs Your Pipes Are Reaching The End

Common signs

  • Rusty or brown water from the tap
  • Pinhole leaks that keep appearing
  • Low pressure at several fixtures
  • Galvanized or polybutylene pipe visible in the basement or crawlspace
  • A history of repairs in the same line

One leak is a repair. A pattern of leaks is a warning. If you are paying for repair after repair, ask for a repipe plan so you can compare costs.

Materials

Which Pipe Replaces The Old Pipe

PEX

  • Flexible and fast to install
  • Handles freeze swings better than rigid pipe
  • Fewer fittings and joints
  • Not for outdoor sun exposure

Copper

  • Long track record
  • Rigid and soldered
  • More labor to install
  • Some water chemistry can cause pinholes

Either can be a good choice. The right answer depends on the house, the water and your budget. We explain the trade offs and you choose.

Local Notes

Lead And Older Homes In Lehi

Lehi City says homes built before 1986 are more likely to have lead pipe or lead solder in the plumbing. Its culinary water system went through a lead free piping project in 1989. If your house is older, ask the city about lead testing before choosing a repipe plan.

Pinhole leaks in copper and corrosion in galvanized pipe are the more common reasons we see for repiping in older neighborhoods. The camera and pressure tests show where the weak spots are.

The Process

How A Repipe Is Planned To Limit Disruption

Most repipes open the walls in small access holes rather than tearing out whole rooms. The plumber plans the order of work so the kitchen and at least one bathroom can come back online as early as possible. Water is shut off for part of each day, so keep a few jugs of drinking water on hand.

After the new lines are in, the system is pressure tested before the walls are closed. The plumber then flushes the lines and checks every fixture for leaks. Faucet aerators can collect loose debris for a few days, so clean them if the flow drops.

Ask who patches the drywall and who paints. Plans differ between companies, and a written scope avoids surprises. We explain this with the price before work starts.

Repiping is governed by the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as amended by the state of Utah, along with the pipe maker instructions for the material you choose.
