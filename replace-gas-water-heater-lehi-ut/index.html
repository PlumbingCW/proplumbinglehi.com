How To Replace A Gas Water Heater In Lehi, UT

A gas water heater swap has seven parts, and the gas connection, venting and strapping are the ones that cannot be rushed.

By the Lehi Pro Plumbing team · Published September 8, 2026

Talk to a plumber
(385) 488-1892
☎ Call Now

Replacing a gas water heater takes seven stages: shut off gas and water, drain the tank, disconnect the old unit, set the new tank on a pan, connect water, gas and the flue, fill and purge, then light and test. Most of it is plumbing work a careful person can follow. The gas connection and the flue are the two parts where a mistake can hurt someone.

This guide walks through each stage so you know what a good job looks like, even if a licensed plumber does the work. If you want it done today, our water heater replacement service is available around the clock.

First Things First

Safety Comes Before Any Wrench

Stop and call a pro if any of these are true

  • You smell gas or hear a hiss near the heater
  • The flue pipe is rusted, loose or disconnected
  • You are not sure the gas shutoff closes fully
  • The tank is in a closet with no clear vent path
  • You have no experience with gas fittings

If you smell gas, leave the building first. Then call 911 and the gas utility. Enbridge Gas lists 1-800-767-1689 for gas emergencies in Utah. Do not flip switches or use a phone inside until the building is cleared.

Step By Step

The Seven Stages Of A Swap

1

Shut off

Close the gas valve on the supply line and the cold water inlet. Switch the control to off.

2

Drain

Attach a hose to the drain valve, open a hot tap upstairs and let the tank empty outdoors or into a floor drain.

3

Disconnect

Unscrew the gas flex line, disconnect the water lines and lift the flue pipe from the draft hood.

4

Set the new tank

Place the tank on a drain pan and level it. Strap it in the upper and lower thirds.

1

Connect water

Use new flex connectors or copper with dielectric fittings where dissimilar metals meet.

2

Connect gas and flue

Use a new gas connector, apply approved thread sealant and check every joint for leaks.

3

Fill and purge

Open the cold inlet and a hot tap and let the tank fill before turning the gas on.

4

Light and test

Follow the label to light the unit, check the flame and make sure the flue draws.

Never fire a gas heater with an empty tank. The burner can damage the tank in minutes and the repair is not covered by most warranties.

Where Mistakes Happen

Gas Joints And The Flue Are The Hard Part

Gas joints must be sealed with sealant rated for gas, not tape made for water lines. After tightening, brush soapy water over each joint. Bubbles mean a leak and the joint needs to be redone before the unit is lit.

The flue carries exhaust out of the house. It needs a continuous upward slope, tight joints and a clear termination. A flue that backdrafts can put carbon monoxide into the living space, so a working CO alarm near the heater is not optional.

Utah Details

Strapping, Pans And Relief Valves

Utah statewide amendments to the plumbing code call for strapping in the upper and lower thirds of the tank. The same amendments set rules for drain pans and the pan drain line where a leak could damage finished space. The relief valve discharge pipe should end where hot water cannot hurt anyone.

Decision Point

When A Replacement Is Smarter Than A Repair

If the tank leaks from its body, replacement is the only fix. If it is only a thermostat, valve or pilot, a repair may be enough. Our water heater repair page has a table of symptoms and likely fixes.

If you are planning a swap in advance, ask whether the same location still makes sense. Moving the unit changes gas piping and venting, and the plan is easier to price when everyone knows the scope.
