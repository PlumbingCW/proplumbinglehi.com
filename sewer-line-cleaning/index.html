If Sewage Is Backing Up, Do This First

1

Stop using water

Do not flush, run the dishwasher or wash clothes. Every gallon adds to the backup.

2

Protect people and pets

Keep children and animals away from affected floors and fixtures.

3

Causes

Why Main Lines Block

The main line carries everything from the house to the sewer. Grease, wipes and paper towels build up, roots work into joints and a sagging section holds debris. When water cannot pass, every fixture lower than the blockage can back up.

The usual pattern is the lowest drain overflowing first, often a basement floor drain or tub. That is a useful clue because it tells the plumber where to start.

Homeowner Tip

Finding Your Cleanout

A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives direct access to the main line. In many homes it sits outside near the foundation or in the basement or garage. Knowing where yours is makes an emergency faster.

If you cannot find one, the plumber can pull a toilet to reach the line. We may recommend adding a cleanout during a repair so future cleanings are simpler.
