No More Clogged Drains: A Practical Guide For Lehi Homes

Most clogs come from habits and small buildup. A few changes and the right fix at the right time keep drains flowing.

By the Lehi Pro Plumbing team · Published November 12, 2026

Talk to a plumber
(385) 488-1892
☎ Call Now

Most clogged drains come from the same few causes: grease in the kitchen, hair and soap in the bathroom, wipes in the toilet and roots or buildup in the main line. You stop them by controlling what goes down, cleaning drains on a schedule and fixing the line when the same clog comes back. A drain that clogs once is a nuisance. A drain that clogs repeatedly is telling you something.

This guide covers prevention, simple fixes and the point where a plumber with a camera is the better call. When you are ready, our drain cleaning service clears kitchen, bath and main lines any hour.

Common Causes

Where Clogs Start

DrainCommon causePrevention
Kitchen sinkGrease, coffee grounds and food scrapsWipe pans, scrape plates, use a sink strainer
Bathroom sinkHair, toothpaste and soapUse a stopper catcher and flush weekly
Shower or tubHair and soap scumUse a drain screen and clean it each week
ToiletWipes and too much paperFlush toilet paper only
Main lineRoots, grease and wipesHabits above and a camera look if clogs return

The kitchen is the biggest source of stubborn clogs. Grease cools in the pipe and sticks to the wall, trapping other debris behind it.

Prevention

Habits That Keep Drains Open

Simple routines that work

  • Pour used grease into a can, not the drain
  • Run hot water for twenty seconds after washing dishes
  • Use a hair catcher in every shower and tub
  • Flush only toilet paper
  • Clean pop up stoppers and strainers monthly

Chemical drain cleaners are tempting but often make things worse. They can sit in a stopped drain, harm pipes and burn skin during later work. A plunger or a hand auger is a safer first move.

First Moves

Fixes To Try Before You Call

1

Plunge

Use a cup plunger on sinks and a flange plunger on toilets.

2

Clean the trap

Place a bucket and unscrew the trap under a sink to remove debris.

3

Use a hand auger

A small auger can reach hair and soap in a branch line.

4

Flush with hot water

Hot water helps move softened grease along.

If a fix works for a day and the drain slows again, the buildup is deeper in the pipe. That is the moment to ask for a cable machine or jetting.

Know The Signs

When To Call A Plumber

Call when

  • More than one drain is slow at the same time
  • Toilets gurgle when a sink drains
  • A floor drain smells or backs up
  • The same drain clogs more than once in a few months
  • You see water or sewage coming up in a tub

A plumber uses larger cable machines and, if needed, a camera and hydro jetting to clean the full pipe wall. For repeat main line clogs, a camera shows the cause.

Local Note

Lehi City And Your Line

The sewer main belongs to the city, and the pipe from your house to the main is on your property. Lehi Water and Sewer at (385) 201-1700 can tell you where the city main begins. If neighbors are also backing up, call the city.

Lehi runs a separate pressurized irrigation system that shuts down for winter in mid October. A wet yard in November is an irrigation or plumbing problem, not a drain problem, so sort out the water source before cabling the line.

Long Term

Cleaning Versus Repairing

Cleaning clears the blockage. Repair fixes the cause. If roots keep entering the line or a section sags, cleaning is a stopgap. A sewer camera inspection shows whether repair is needed.

Reading Symptoms

Which Drain Is Telling You Something

One slow sink usually has a local clog in the trap or the branch pipe. Several slow fixtures at once point to the main line. A drain that gurgles when another fixture runs suggests a vent or main line problem.

Hair and soap catch in bathroom drains. Grease builds up in kitchen lines. Pouring hot grease down the sink only moves the problem down the pipe until it cools, so wipe pans with a paper towel first.

Chemical drain cleaners can harm older pipes and are hazardous for anyone who later opens the trap. A drain snake or a plumber is a safer tool.

Tools

Choosing A Professional Method

A cable machine suits most household clogs and clears roots or buildup from a section of pipe. A camera shows the line and helps tell a clog from a break. Hydro jetting uses high pressure water to scrub grease and scale from pipe walls, and we explain when it fits before using it.

If a drain clogs again within weeks, tell the plumber. A repeat clog is information about what is happening in the line.
