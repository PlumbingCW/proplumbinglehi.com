How To Stop A Toilet From Clogging Again And Again In Lehi

Frequent toilet clogs have a cause: what goes in, the flush itself or the line beyond it. Here is how to tell which one.

By the Lehi Pro Plumbing team · Published November 11, 2026

Talk to a plumber
(385) 488-1892
☎ Call Now

A toilet that clogs repeatedly usually has one of four causes: items that should not be flushed, a weak flush from clogged jets or low tank water, a partial blockage in the trap, or a problem in the line beyond the toilet. You can often rule out the first two on your own. The last two need a plumber with a camera.

If other drains in the house are slow too, the cause is deeper than the toilet, and our sewer line cleaning service is the next step. If it is only the toilet, start with the checks below.

Habits

What Should Never Be Flushed

Keep these out of the toilet

  • Wipes of any kind, even ones labeled flushable
  • Paper towels and tissues made for faces
  • Cotton swabs and balls
  • Feminine products and diapers
  • Dental floss and hair

Wipes are the leading cause of repeat blockages in lines, because they do not break down like toilet paper. A single wipe can snag on a rough spot and trap more behind it.

Quick Checks

Test The Flush

CheckWhat to look forWhat it suggests
Water level in the tankShould be at the fill lineLow water means a weak flush
Rim jets under the bowl lipShould be clear and flowingClogged jets weaken the swirl
Bowl water level after a flushShould return to normal quicklySlow refill points to a partial blockage
Gurgling in a nearby sink or tubShould be silentGurgling points to a line or vent problem

Pour a bucket of water into the bowl. If it drains quickly, the trap is probably clear. If it rises slowly, the trap or the line is partly blocked.

DIY Steps

Simple Fixes To Try

1

Use a flange plunger

A flange plunger seals better in a toilet than a flat cup.

2

Clean the rim jets

Use a small brush or a wire to clear mineral buildup in the jets.

3

Check the tank water level

Adjust the fill valve if the water is low.

4

Try a closet auger

A toilet auger reaches the trap without scratching the porcelain.

Avoid chemical drain cleaners in a toilet. They can sit in the bowl, damage the finish and splash skin during later work.

Beyond The Toilet

When The Problem Is Deeper

A camera shows what a cable cannot. Roots, a belly in the pipe, a cracked section or a buildup of paper can all cause repeat clogs. If a plumber cables the line and it clogs again within weeks, ask for a sewer camera inspection.

Septic systems add another cause. A full tank or a failing drain field can back up toilets first. If you are on septic, call a septic pumper before you spend money on cabling.

Local Note

Sewer And Septic Notes For Lehi

A shared utility, the Timpanogos Special Service District, treats sewage for Lehi and its neighbors. Parts of Lehi are on septic instead. Septic owners are advised to pump every three to five years and keep wipes and other non degradable items out of the system.

If neighbors on your street are also backing up, call Lehi Water and Sewer at (385) 201-1700 during office hours, because the city main may be the problem.

Hardware

Toilet Types And Flush Strength

Older low flow toilets can have weak flushes, especially on long drain runs. If the toilet is old and keeps failing, replacement may be the better fix. See our toilet repair page for how we decide between repair and replacement.

Toilet and drain calls come in from Lehi and American Fork alike. Look at the service area page to see whether your street is covered.

Prevention

Habits That Keep The Line Clear

Only toilet paper and human waste belong in the bowl. Wipes labeled flushable still clog lines, and so do feminine products, paper towels and cotton swabs. Keep a small covered bin near the toilet and tell guests where it is.

Use less paper per flush and flush twice after a large load. A slow flush that worsens over weeks suggests a partial blockage past the trap or a vent problem, not a one time clog.

A plunger with a flange fits a toilet better than a flat cup. Seal the drain opening and push with steady strokes. If the water does not move after a few tries, stop and call, because repeated forcing can push waste back.

Warning Signs

When It Is More Than A Clog

If every toilet in the house is slow, or water backs up in a tub when you flush, the problem is probably in the main line and not the bowl. Stop running water and call. A sewer inspection camera can show whether roots or a break are involved.

Toys and too much paper are the usual suspects in a single toilet. An older pipe with a low spot can collect paper in the same place again and again.
