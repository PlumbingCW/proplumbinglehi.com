Emergency Response

Water In The Pit And The Pump Is Silent

When water is rising in the pit and the pump is not running, you have minutes, not hours. Check that the pump is plugged in and the breaker has not tripped. Do not step into standing water near outlets, and call the 24/7 line if the pit is filling.

1

Check power

Confirm the plug and the breaker. A tripped GFCI outlet is a common cause.

2

Lift stored items

Move boxes, electronics and anything on the floor away from the pit.

3

Bail if safe

A bucket buys time. Stay out of water near outlets or the panel.

4

Call the 24/7 line

We will triage the pump and plan the visit.

Causes

Why Sump Pumps Fail

FaultSignFix
Stuck float switchPump will not start or will not stopReposition or replace the switch
Burned out motorHums or silent, water risesReplace the pump
Clogged intakeRuns but pumps little waterClean the pit and intake
Failed check valveWater returns after each cycleReplace the valve
Frozen or blocked dischargePump runs but water does not leaveClear the line and reroute if needed
Power lossPump stops in a stormAdd a battery backup

Most pumps are replaced rather than repaired once the motor fails. A new pump is also a good time to check the discharge path and the check valve.

Choosing A Pump

Pump Types

Pedestal pump

  • Motor sits above the pit
  • Easier to reach and service
  • Louder in operation
  • Often a lower upfront cost

Submersible pump

  • Sits in the water, quieter
  • Handles heavier flow
  • Cooler motor in a closed pit
  • Harder to reach for service
Backup Systems

Backups For When The Power Goes Out

Storms that flood basements often knock out power. A battery backup pump sits beside the main pump and takes over when the main pump fails or the power drops. Water powered backups also exist where the water supply allows.

A backup is not a replacement for a working main pump. It buys hours of protection and gives you time to call us.

Local Notes

Winter And Snow Melt In Lehi

Spring snow melt sends a lot of water toward foundations, and sump pumps run hardest then. Discharge lines can freeze in winter and block the pump outlet, so we check where the line exits and how it drains. Water should discharge away from the foundation, not back toward it.

Utah Department of Environmental Quality materials passed along by Lehi City say one in three Utah homes tests above the EPA radon action level of 4 pCi per liter. A sump pit is a path for radon gas, so ask a radon professional whether a sealed lid helps. We can seal the lid at the plumbing side.

Related Equipment

Sewage Ejector Pumps

Basement bathrooms below the sewer line use an ejector pump instead of a sump pump. These pumps handle waste, not groundwater, and their failure is a sewage problem. Call right away if an ejector pit alarm sounds or fixtures back up.
