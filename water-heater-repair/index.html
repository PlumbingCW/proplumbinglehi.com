Start With What You See

Most water heater calls start with one of three things: no hot water, not enough hot water, or water on the floor. Each has a short list of causes, and the water on the floor is the one that cannot wait.

Water On The Floor

Leak Calls Come First

Water heater leaks are the biggest group of calls we see for this service. The first move is the same each time. Close the cold water inlet on top of the tank, turn the gas control to pilot or switch off the electric breaker, and call.

A leak from a fitting or relief valve is often a repair. A leak from the bottom of the tank is different. Once the steel wall corrodes through, the tank cannot be saved, and water heater replacement is the honest answer.

Check these before you call

  • Is the breaker on, or is the gas valve open and the pilot lit?
  • Is the relief valve discharge pipe dripping?
  • Is there water pooling under the tank or only on a pipe?
  • Is there a rotten egg smell? If yes, leave and call the gas utility first
Know The Difference

Gas Heater Faults And Electric Heater Faults

Gas tank heaters

  • Pilot light will not stay lit
  • Thermocouple or flame sensor worn
  • Gas control valve faults
  • Venting blocked or backdrafting

Electric tank heaters

  • Upper or lower element burned out
  • Thermostat stuck or failed
  • Breaker tripping repeatedly
  • Wiring burned at the terminals

We carry the common parts for these repairs on the truck. If a part is not in stock, the plumber explains the wait and the options before you approve the visit.

Code Notes

Utah Rules We Follow On Water Heaters

Utah adds statewide amendments to the plumbing code that cover how a water heater is strapped and drained. The amendments call for strapping in the upper and lower thirds of the tank and for a drain pan with a proper drain line where a leak could damage the floor below.

Lehi City also publishes a brochure on thermal expansion in water heaters. We check for an expansion tank on closed systems, because pressure that spikes after each heating cycle wears out relief valves and fittings.
