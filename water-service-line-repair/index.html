The Basics

Where Your Water Service Line Runs

Meter and box

Lehi City owns the meter. Do not operate the city valve at the curb unless the city asks you to.

Service line

The pipe from the meter to the house is your responsibility after the meter.

Main shutoff

A valve where the line enters the house lets you stop water inside.

Pressure regulator

Many homes have a regulator near the entry point that protects fixtures.

Knowing which part belongs to whom saves time. A leak before the meter is the city side. A leak after the meter is yours to fix.

Warning Signs

Signs Of A Failing Water Line

What to look and listen for

  • A wet or sunken patch in the yard along the line
  • A meter that keeps moving with fixtures off
  • Low pressure throughout the house
  • Water in the meter box
  • Discolored water after a pressure change

A buried leak can run for weeks without surfacing. The first sign is often a bill that climbs. The meter test on our leak detection page shows how to check.

Emergency Response

Local Notes

Lehi Ground And Water Facts

Lehi gets culinary water from wells, springs and wholesale surface water from the Central Utah Water Conservancy District, and the city treats it with chlorine. The pressurized irrigation system is separate and carries untreated water. A buried line that feeds the house is not the same as a buried line that feeds the yard, and we sort out which is which before digging.
