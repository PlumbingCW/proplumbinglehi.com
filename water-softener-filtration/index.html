Test First, Then Buy

Lehi City supplies culinary water from wells, springs and wholesale surface water from the Central Utah Water Conservancy District, and treats it with chlorine. The reports we reviewed list sodium, sulfate and total dissolved solids ranges, but they do not publish a hardness number.

Because we cannot quote hardness for your tap, we do not guess. Use a home test kit or a lab test, then bring the result to us. The right equipment depends on the number, and a wrong guess wastes money.

Compare

Salt Softener Or Salt Free

Salt based softener

  • Removes hardness minerals
  • Uses salt and a drain connection
  • Adds a small amount of sodium
  • Needs a bag of salt added regularly

Salt free conditioner

  • Changes how scale forms
  • No salt or drain needed
  • Does not remove minerals
  • Different results by water type

If sodium is a concern for you, ask your doctor and tell us. We can recommend an option that fits.

Local Safety

Lead, Older Homes And Drinking Water

The city publishes lead and copper sampling results and points to pre 1986 homes as the ones most likely to have lead in house plumbing. If your home is older and you are concerned, ask the city about testing. A filter certified for lead reduction can be a stopgap, and a repipe is the lasting fix.

What To Expect

Installation Details

A whole house system needs space near the main line, a drain for regeneration, a bypass and sometimes an electrical outlet. We plan the location so you can reach the tank and the salt. Many Lehi homes have a garage or basement utility room with enough space.

Choosing Equipment

Choosing Between Softening And Filtering

A softener removes the minerals that cause scale. A filter deals with taste, odor or sediment. They solve different problems, so the first step is to test your tap and decide which problem you actually have.

Salt based softeners need a drain connection for regeneration and a regular supply of salt. Equipment is sized to household water use, not just the number of people. We size the equipment after you have a test result in hand.
